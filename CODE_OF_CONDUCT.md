# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

The Open Patterns Initiative is a practitioner community. We value:

- **Honesty over polish.** Share what broke, not just what worked.
- **Substance over performance.** Patterns are judged on technical merit, not presentation.
- **Respect.** Disagree on approaches, not on people.
- **Vendor neutrality.** Reference tools; don't sell them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Marketing disguised as pattern contributions
- Personal attacks or dismissive language in reviews
- Sharing proprietary information without authorization
- Plagiarizing patterns without attribution

## Enforcement

Violations will be addressed by the Cloud Nirvana team. Repeated violations result in removal from the contributor list.
